# Modals

Structure

- **Affirmative:** subject + modal (+ ...) + main verb
- **Negative:** subject + modal + not (+ ...) + main verb
- **Question:** modal + subject (+ ...) + main verb


| Modal verb   | Example sentense                                             | Meaning/Function             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| **Can**      | Everyone can pursue their passions.                          | Ability/Permission           |
| **Might**    | Conflicts might be resolved through dialogue.                | Possibility                  |
| **Would**    | Would people willingly share their resources?                | Hypothetical/conditional     |
| **Would**    | Individuals would have the freedom to pursue their interests | Hypothetical/Conditional     |
| **Will**     | Will there be a need for strict regulations?                 | Future/Probability           |
| **May**      | People may experience a profound sense of fulfillment.       | Possibility/Permission       |
| **Could**    | Could technology enhance the quality of life?                | Possbility/Ability           |
| **Should**   | Citizens should participate actively.                        | Obligation/Advice            |
| **Ought to** | Individuals ought to respect diversity.                      | Moral Obligation/Expectation |
| **Must**     | Every member must contribute to the common good              | Necessity/requirement        |
| **Have to**  | Citizens have to take responsibility for cleanliness         | Obligation/Necessity         |
| **Shall**    | Shall we envision a utopian society?                         | Suggestion/Proposal          |
